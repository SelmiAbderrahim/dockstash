# Digital PR Campaign Templates

Reusable templates for Phase 7 digital PR: data-led campaign brief, reactive-PR pitch templates, the E-E-A-T checklist, and the semantic-consensus tracker. Examples use Dockstash (one-click Restic-based Docker backup; ICP = solo devs, indie hackers, small teams, agencies on Docker Compose). Consistent brand attributes to reinforce in every mention: **one-click**, **Restic-based**, **built for Docker Compose**, **tested/verifiable restores**, **for solo devs & small teams**.

## Data campaign brief template

```
CAMPAIGN NAME: [e.g., "The State of Self-Hosted Backups 2026"]

OBJECTIVE: Earn Tier-1/Tier-2 editorial links + AI citation authority.

DATASET SOURCE:
- [ ] Proprietary product data  [ ] Original survey  [ ] Novel analysis of public data
Description: [what the data is, sample size, time period]

METHODOLOGY (publish this — it's an E-E-A-T signal):
[How data was collected, sample size, dates, any caveats.]

THE 3-5 FINDINGS (surprising, timely, or superlative):
1. [HEADLINE finding — the single most compelling stat; this is the hook]
2. [supporting finding]
3. [supporting finding]
4. [optional]
5. [optional]

LEAD STAT (the story hook):
"[e.g., 63% of self-hosted Docker projects have no working backup at all]"

LINKABLE ASSET:
- URL: [report/landing page]
- Format: [report page + charts + downloadable data]
- Contains: headline stat, method, charts, quotable spokesperson line

SPOKESPERSON: [Name, Title] — available for quotes/interviews

TARGET PUBLICATIONS: [see publication tiers below]

BRAND ATTRIBUTES TO REINFORCE: one-click, Restic-based, Docker Compose, tested restores
```

**Formula reminder:** dataset → extract 3-5 surprising findings → lead with the most compelling → journalists cite it → editorial backlinks.

## Reactive PR pitch templates

### A) Journalist-request response (Qwoted / Featured)

Respond fast — deadline-driven journalists reward speed. Keep it tight and immediately quotable.

```
Subject: Expert quote — [topic of their request]

Hi [Journalist],

[One-sentence credential establishing why you're a relevant expert.]

Quote you can use as-is:
"[2-3 sentences of genuine, specific insight. Quotable, no fluff, adds a
real expert angle — not a product pitch.]"
— [Full Name], [Title], [Company]

Happy to expand, get on a call before your deadline, or share supporting data.

[Name] | [Title] | [Company] | [link] | [contact]
```

### B) Cold pitch for a data-led campaign

```
Subject: [Surprising lead stat] — new data on [topic]

Hi [Journalist],

New data point that fits [their beat/recent article]: [lead stat].

We surveyed [N] [audience] and found [1-2 sentence summary of the finding
and why it's surprising/timely].

Full findings + methodology + charts: [linkable asset URL]
[Spokesperson Name, Title] is available for a quote or interview.

Would this be useful for a piece?

[Name] | [Title] | [Company] | [link]
```

Pitch rules: personalize to the journalist's beat, lead with the news value (not your product), keep it short, offer data + a spokesperson, and make the asset easy to cite.

## E-E-A-T checklist

Verify each earned mention and each linkable asset reinforces Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness:

- [ ] **Experience** — content reflects first-hand/product-derived experience (real usage data, real restores)
- [ ] **Expertise** — quotes attributed to a named, credentialed spokesperson (name + title)
- [ ] **Authoritativeness** — placement is in a publication your buyers respect (Tier-1/Tier-2, relevant beat)
- [ ] **Trustworthiness** — methodology published, claims verifiable, no exaggeration
- [ ] Natural, contextual anchor (brand name or descriptive — not exact-match stuffing)
- [ ] Brand attributes described consistently with all other placements
- [ ] Relevance: the linking page is topically related to backups/DevOps/containers

One placement that passes all of these outweighs dozens of directory links.

## Semantic-consensus tracker

Log every earned mention so you can see authority convergence build across trusted sources. Hand this to the ai-seo skill.

| Publication | Tier | Link/anchor | one-click | Restic-based | Docker Compose | tested restores | for small teams | Date |
|-------------|:----:|-------------|:---------:|:------------:|:--------------:|:---------------:|:---------------:|------|
| [pub name]  |  1   | [anchor]    |           |              |                |                 |                 |      |

Mark the attribute columns each mention reinforces. When multiple **high-trust** publications describe the brand with the **same attributes**, AI models read that as authority consensus and begin recommending the brand. Consistency across Tier-1 sources is the highest-weight signal available.

## Target publication tiers

| Tier | Description | Dockstash examples (illustrative) |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Major tech/business press, highest authority | large tech news outlets, major business press |
| **Tier 2** | Respected industry/DevOps publications & newsletters | DevOps/self-hosting publications, well-read dev newsletters |
| **Tier 3** | Niche blogs, community publications, podcasts | homelab/self-hosted community blogs, developer podcasts |

Prioritize Tier 1-2 for authority weight, but relevant Tier-3 placements still reinforce consensus and often convert well because the audience is precisely your ICP. Relevance can outweigh raw authority when the audience match is exact.
