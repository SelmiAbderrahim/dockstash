## Role 1: Growth Marketer

### Title
Growth Marketer (Part-time / Contract)

### About ServerlessBase
ServerlessBase is a SaaS deployment platform that makes shipping applications effortless. We are a solo-founder company augmented by 169 AI agents and 89 skills, on a mission to reach 1,000 users and 100 Pro subscribers ($29/mo) within 12 months. You will be the first human hire focused entirely on growth.

### Role
Own the growth engine: acquisition channels, conversion funnels, and retention loops. You will set strategy and direction; AI agents handle content generation, SEO implementation, email sequence writing, and analytics reporting. Your job is to think, prioritize, and make judgment calls that AI cannot.

### Responsibilities
- Design and prioritize growth experiments (minimum 3 per week)
- Define channel strategy: SEO, content marketing, paid ads, partnerships, community
- Analyze funnel data and identify conversion bottlenecks
- Brief AI agents on content topics, ad copy, and email sequences
- Review and approve AI-generated marketing assets
- Own CAC, conversion rate, and WAD growth metrics
- Report weekly to the founder on growth performance

### Requirements
- 2+ years in B2B SaaS growth marketing (developer tools preferred)
- Demonstrated ability to take a product from 0 to 1,000 users
- Comfortable working with AI tools as primary execution layer
- Strong analytical skills; able to read dashboards and derive actions
- Experience with at least 3 of: SEO, content marketing, paid ads, email, community
- Self-directed; thrives without a large team or established processes

### AI Tools You Will Use
- 32 marketing skills (copywriting, SEO audit, content strategy, paid ads, etc.)
- AI content generation agents for blog posts, social media, ad creative
- Analytics tracking agents for GA4/Mixpanel event setup
- A/B test design agents for experiment planning
- Competitor analysis agents for positioning research

### Compensation Range
- Contract: $3,000-$5,000/month for 15-20 hours/week
- Full-time (if converted): $70,000-$90,000/year + equity (0.25-0.5%)

### Location
Remote (async-first, overlap with UTC+0 to UTC+3 preferred)

## Common Sections for All Roles

### What Makes This Different

You will not join a traditional company. ServerlessBase operates as a solo founder augmented by 169 AI agents. This means:

- **Speed**: Decisions happen in hours, not weeks
- **AI-native**: You will work alongside AI agents daily, not as a novelty but as core workflow
- **Ownership**: You will own your entire function from day one
- **Impact**: Your work directly moves the revenue needle; no organizational layers between you and outcomes
- **Learning**: You will develop AI-augmented work skills that are increasingly valuable in the market

### Benefits

- Fully remote, async-first work
- Flexible hours (results-oriented, not hours-oriented)
- Equipment stipend ($1,000 for initial setup)
- AI tool access (all company AI agents and skills available for your work)
- Equity participation (vesting: 4 years, 1-year cliff)
- Conference/learning budget ($500/year)
