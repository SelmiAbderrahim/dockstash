# Terms of Service Requirements 2025

## Legal Framework

### Enforceability Requirements
- Clear acceptance mechanism (clickwrap preferred)
- Conspicuous presentation
- Opportunity to review before acceptance
- Record of acceptance

### SaaS-Specific Considerations
- Service availability commitments
- Data ownership clarity
- Intellectual property rights
- Third-party integrations

## Required Sections

### 1. Acceptance of Terms

**Must include:**
- Agreement to be bound
- Eligibility requirements (age, capacity)
- Authority to bind organization
- Acceptance of Privacy Policy

**Example:**
```
By accessing or using ServerlessBase, you agree to be bound by these Terms.
If you are using ServerlessBase on behalf of an organization, you represent
that you have authority to bind that organization to these Terms.
```

### 2. Service Description

**Define clearly:**
- What the service provides
- Features and capabilities
- Limitations
- Supported platforms/browsers

### 3. Account Terms

**Account requirements:**
- Registration requirements
- Credential security
- Account sharing policy
- Account suspension/termination

**Responsibilities:**
- Accurate information
- Secure credentials
- Prompt notification of unauthorized access

### 4. Acceptable Use Policy

**Permitted uses:**
- Intended purpose
- Authorized users
- Geographic restrictions (if any)

**Prohibited activities:**
- Illegal activities
- Harmful content
- Security violations
- Resource abuse
- Reverse engineering
- Competitive analysis

### 5. Billing and Payments

**Pricing:**
- Current pricing structure
- Currency
- Taxes and fees

**Billing:**
- Billing cycles (monthly, annual)
- Payment methods
- Auto-renewal terms
- Failed payment handling

**Refunds:**
- Refund policy
- Pro-rata calculations
- Non-refundable items

**Cancellation:**
- Cancellation process
- Notice period (30 days typical)
- Effect on access
- Data handling post-cancellation

### 6. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Uptime commitment:**
- Target uptime (e.g., 99.9%)
- Calculation method
- Measurement period

**Service credits:**
- Credit calculation
- Claim process
- Maximum credits

**Exclusions:**
- Scheduled maintenance
- Force majeure
- User-caused issues
- Third-party failures

### 7. Intellectual Property

**Our IP:**
- Platform ownership
- Trademarks
- Patents (if applicable)
- License scope

**User license:**
- What users can do
- Restrictions
- Duration

### 8. User Content

**Ownership:**
- User retains ownership
- License granted to us

**License terms:**
- Non-exclusive
- Worldwide
- Royalty-free
- For service provision

**Responsibility:**
- User responsible for content
- No obligation to monitor
- Right to remove

### 9. Third-Party Services

**Integrations:**
- Third-party services used
- Terms applicability
- No liability for third parties

**Linked services:**
- External links disclaimer
- User responsibility

### 10. Limitation of Liability

**Liability caps:**
- Maximum liability (e.g., fees paid in 12 months)
- Exclusion of consequential damages
- Exceptions (gross negligence, willful misconduct)

**Disclaimer:**
- "As is" provision
- No warranty of merchantability
- No warranty of fitness for purpose

### 11. Indemnification

**User indemnification:**
- Content indemnification
- Misuse indemnification
- Third-party claims

**Process:**
- Notification requirement
- Control of defense
- Settlement approval

### 12. Termination

**Our termination rights:**
- Breach of terms
- Illegal activity
- Non-payment
- At will (with notice)

**User termination rights:**
- At any time
- Process

**Effect of termination:**
- Access cessation
- Data retention period
- Surviving provisions

### 13. Dispute Resolution

**Governing law:**
- Applicable jurisdiction
- Choice of law

**Dispute process:**
- Informal resolution first
- Arbitration clause (if applicable)
- Class action waiver
- Opt-out provision (30 days)

**International users:**
- Local law compliance
- Export restrictions

### 14. Modifications to Terms

**Change process:**
- Right to modify
- Notice period (30 days for material changes)
- Notification method
- Continued use = acceptance

### 15. General Provisions

**Standard clauses:**
- Entire agreement
- Severability
- Waiver
- Assignment
- No third-party beneficiaries
- Force majeure

### 16. Contact Information

**Required:**
- Legal contact email
- Physical address
- Support contact

## SaaS-Specific Clauses

### Data Processing
- Data processing agreement reference
- Data controller/processor relationship
- Subprocessor list

### API Terms
- API usage limits
- Rate limiting
- API changes notice

### Multi-Tenant Considerations
- Data isolation
- Resource allocation
- Fair use policy

### AI/ML Terms (if applicable)
- AI feature usage
- Training data policy
- Opt-out provisions
