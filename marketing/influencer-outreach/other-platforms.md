# Other Platforms

Same offer, same honesty, different etiquette. Reddit and YouTube will both
punish you for getting the form wrong even when the substance is fine.

---

## Reddit

Reddit is the highest-value and highest-risk channel here: r/selfhosted,
r/homelab, r/docker, and r/devops are exactly our audience, and all four will
shred an account that arrives selling something.

**Read these before you do anything:**

- Never post an invite link in a thread or a comment. Single-use links die on the
  first click, and self-promo rules will kill the post anyway.
- Most of these subs require a participation history. Comment usefully for weeks
  before you DM anyone. If your account's first action is outreach, you've
  already lost.
- Check each sub's self-promotion rule and its "vendor" flair policy. Some
  require you to identify as the developer in every comment where you mention
  your own product. Do that unprompted, every time.
- If you want to talk about Dockstash in a thread, modmail the mods first and ask
  what's allowed. The answer is often "post it, but flair it and disclose."

**The DM** (to a specific user whose comment you're continuing, not a broadcast):

> Hey {name} — you wrote {content_reference} in r/{subreddit}, and that
> trade-off is what I've been stuck on too.
>
> I'm the developer of Dockstash (disclosing up front). It backs up a Docker
> project with restic and then runs a scheduled restore drill — restores the
> snapshot into a throwaway container to prove it's real.
>
> I'd like to hand you a free year of the Business plan. Single-use link, no
> card, no post required, lapses to Free after 12 months: {invite_link}
>
> If you try it and it's worse than your current setup, telling me exactly where
> is worth more to me than a mention.

**Modmail** (only if you want to post about the product itself, not to reach a
user):

> Hi mods — I'm the developer of Dockstash, a Docker backup tool with automated
> restore drills. I'd like to post about it in r/{subreddit} and I'd rather ask
> than guess.
>
> Happy to flair it, disclose in every comment, and stay in the thread to answer
> technical questions. If self-promo posts aren't welcome here at all, tell me
> and I'll just keep commenting on other people's threads.
>
> — Selmi

Never DM a mod team member individually about this. Use modmail.

## YouTube creators

Comments are worthless for this. Find the **business inquiry email** — the
"View email address" button on the channel's About tab — and write an email.
Subject lines matter more than the body.

Label convention: `yt-<handle>`.

**Subject:** `Free year of Dockstash — no video required`

That subject is doing two things: it names the offer, and it removes the
transaction before they open the mail. Do not write "Collaboration Opportunity"
or "Sponsorship Inquiry" — those go to a folder.

**Body:**

> Hi {name},
>
> {content_reference}
>
> I build Dockstash. It backs up a whole Docker project — volumes, databases,
> configs — with restic, and then runs a scheduled restore drill: it restores the
> snapshot into a throwaway container and verifies it actually comes back. Most
> backup tools tell you the backup succeeded. This one tells you the restore
> succeeded.
>
> I'd like to give you a free year of the Business plan. There's no video
> required, no script, no approval process, and no contract. It's a single-use
> link, it works once, and the twelve months start whenever you activate it:
>
> {invite_link}
>
> If you do end up covering it, you'd need to disclose that you got the year for
> free — I'd rather that disclosure be boring and up front. And if you point it
> at your own stack and it falls over, please tell me. That's the more useful
> outcome for me.
>
> Selmi
> dockstash.com

**Follow-up:** one, at day 8. Creators' business inboxes are landfills; a single
polite bump is fair, a second is not.

> Hi {name} — bumping this once in case it got buried. The link is still live
> ({expiry_date}) and it's the only one I minted for you. If it's not a fit, no
> reply needed.
>
> {invite_link}

low-key, allergic to marketing voice, quick to reply to something specific.

Label convention: `bs-<handle>`.

> Hi {name} — {content_reference}

> restore drill, so you find out the restore works before you need it to.
>
> Free year of the Business plan, single-use link, no card, no post required:

## Universal rules

These hold on every platform above.

1. **The invite link never appears in public.** DMs, modmail, and email only.
2. **One link per person.** Reusing a link means the second person gets a dead
   page and a bad first impression.
3. **Disclosure is stated by you, first, unprompted.** Never make the creator
   figure out the ethics.
4. **Two follow-ups maximum, then silence.** (One on Bluesky and YouTube.)
5. **No fabricated scarcity.** The link really is single-use and really expires;
   that's the only urgency you're allowed to claim.
6. **"Try it and share it if you like it" — never "post about us."** If somebody
   asks what they owe you, the answer is nothing.
