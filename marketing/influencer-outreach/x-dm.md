# X / Twitter

X DMs have a 10,000-character limit, but attention has a two-sentence limit.
Anything longer than a phone screen gets left on read. Links in DMs are fine;
links in public replies look like spam.

Label convention: `x-<handle>`.

## The DM

> Hey {name} — {content_reference}
>
> I build Dockstash: backs up a whole Docker project with restic, then runs a
> scheduled restore drill that actually restores the snapshot into a throwaway
> container. Backup-succeeded is a lie; restore-succeeded isn't.
>
> Free year of Business, yours, no card: {invite_link}
> Single-use link, expires in 30 days, the year starts when you activate.
>
> No post required. If you do mention it, say you got the year free.
>
> — Selmi

## Follow-up 1 — day 3

> {name} — bumping this once, then I'll stop. Link's still live and it's the only
> one I made for you: {invite_link}
>
> If it's not for you, "not for me" is a complete reply.

## Follow-up 2 — day 8 (last one)

> Last one, promise. Link dies {expiry_date}. If you ever want to watch a restore
> drill fail loudly instead of a backup succeed quietly, it's there.
>
> {invite_link}

## Closed DMs: the public-reply opener

If their DMs are shut, do **not** put the invite link in a public reply — it's
single-use and the first stranger to click burns it.

Reply to a specific, recent post of theirs with something that stands on its own
as a contribution. Then ask to move it to DMs.

> {a real, specific technical response to what they posted — one that would be
> worth posting even if you wanted nothing}
>
> Side note: I build a Docker backup tool that does restore drills, and I'd like
> to give you a free year of it. Open your DMs for a sec and it's yours — nothing
> required in return.

Rules for the public reply:

- The technical content comes first and must be genuinely useful. If you'd delete
  it once you removed the ask, don't send it.
- Never post the link.
- One reply. If they don't open DMs, they said no.
- Don't quote-tweet them to get attention. That's a broadcast, not a DM.

## Length discipline

X strips formatting. Write it so it reads as four short paragraphs on a phone:
hook, what it is, the offer, the release. If you can't make the reference in one
sentence, you haven't read enough of their work to send this.
