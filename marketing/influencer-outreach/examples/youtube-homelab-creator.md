## Why this shape

- The opener quotes *him*, with a timestamp. It's unautomatable and it continues a
  thought he started on camera — the strongest reference class.
- "which is the part you keep meaning to test" ties the differentiator to his own
  sentence. One idea, no feature list.
- YouTubers get 20 sponsorship emails a week; "no video required" is the line that
  separates this from all of them.

## Do not

- Comment the offer publicly on a video. Ever.
- Offer a "rate card" or convert this into a paid integration if he asks — the
  program answer is: free year, no strings; paid placements are a different
  conversation (doc 10), never bundled with this one.
- Send if the backup line is older than ~a year — he's probably fixed it or forgotten.
