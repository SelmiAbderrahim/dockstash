# Case: LinkedIn Agency Technical Lead

**Who:** technical lead / founder at a web agency hosting client sites on their own
VPSes (the doc-09 "Marco" ICP). This is the influencer-program variant — a creator or
visible voice at an agency. Pure sales prospects with no audience go through
`../../linkedin-growth/03-outbound-dm-playbook.md` instead; one person never sits in
both sequences.
**Channel:** LinkedIn — connection note first, DM after accept (never same breath).
**Label:** `li-<handle>`.

## Example prospect (invented — replace everything)

Tomas K., technical director at a 12-person agency, posts weekly about self-hosting
client infrastructure. Recent post: "We moved 30 client sites off shared hosting onto
our own Docker hosts. AMA." Their site's care-plan page says "daily backups included."

## Connection note (≤300 chars, no link)

> Hi Tomas — your "30 client sites onto our own Docker hosts" post is the reason I'm
> connecting. I build Dockstash: Docker backups that run scheduled restore drills to
> prove each client's snapshot restores. No pitch, just same-room. — Selmi

## The DM (after accept)

> Hi Tomas — that AMA thread was good; the answer about margin on self-hosted
> care plans especially.
>
> I build Dockstash. For a fleet like yours it does three specific things: dumps
> each client's database correctly from the running container (not a live file
> copy), backs everything up with restic to your own storage, and runs scheduled
> **restore drills** — so "your backup was restore-tested on {date}" can be a line
> in your monthly client reports instead of a hope. One dashboard across all your
> VPSes, flat pricing instead of per-site.
>
> I'd like you to have a free year of the Business plan (unlimited projects) — no
> card, nothing to cancel, lapses to Free after. This link is yours alone and works
> exactly once: {invite_link}
>
> No post or endorsement required. If you do write about it, please say you got the
> year free. And if it doesn't survive contact with 30 real client stacks, that
> feedback is worth more to me than the mention.
>
> — Selmi

## Follow-ups

Standard `../linkedin-dm.md` cadence: day 3, day 8, stop.

## Why this shape

- The differentiator flexes to the agency pain: *report line + fleet dashboard +
  flat pricing*, not the generic drill pitch. Same product, their problem.
- "Doesn't survive contact with 30 real client stacks" invites the exact stress test
  you want — an agency that runs it at fleet scale and stays is the best case in the
  whole program.

## Do not

- Send the invite link in the connection note (LinkedIn strips/flags it, and it
  reads as spam).
- Let this drift into a sales sequence — no pricing negotiation, no demo-call push.
  If they ask for a demo, of course, yes.
