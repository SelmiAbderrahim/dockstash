# 90-Day Execution Calendar

Operating doc. Assumes ~10 focused hrs/week solo. Weekly recurring block stays constant; phase work layers on top. If a week collapses, protect (in order): community replies → weekly blog post → KPI review. Ads can pause; compounding channels shouldn't.

## Recurring weekly block (~5.5 hrs, starts week 1)

| Slot | Time | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| Community replies (F5Bot alerts, daily 20 min) | 2h | 05 |
| One blog/guide post | 2h | 03 |
| 3–4 social posts (batched) | 1h | 07 |
| KPI dashboard + kill/scale calls | 30m | 11 |

- [ ] robots.txt AI-bots allowed; llms.txt shipped; baseline AI-citation audit (04)

## Day-90 review (2 hrs, honest)

1. Which 2 channels drove 80% of **activated** signups? → month 4 doubles them; cut the bottom half.
2. Funnel health vs targets: visitor→signup ≥2%, activation ≥40%, free→paid 3–8%, churn <5% (11).
3. AI citations: any of the 8 queries citing Dockstash on any platform? (04)
4. CAC per channel vs $30 ceiling → ads verdict (06).
5. MRR reality check: even $200–500 MRR at day 90 with healthy funnel + compounding organic = on track; flat traffic + dead funnel = revisit positioning (01) before spending more anywhere.

## Standing rules

- Nothing ships without UTM (11). Every objection heard anywhere gets logged. Every claim traceable to the product (README ground rules).
- Kill rules are pre-committed (06 ads, 07 LinkedIn, 09 agency ICP) — day-90 you decides with data, not sunk cost.
